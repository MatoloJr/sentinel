# Climate Sentinel Kenya - Action Playbook

> Planning-support only. Actions are taken from the cited guidance documents; the agent may only recommend actions listed here. Official warnings from the Kenya Meteorological Department and the National Disaster Operations Centre take priority.

## Drought

**Watch** - 3-month rain 50-75% of normal (comparable to NDMA 'Alert')

- `DR-W1` Disseminate drought early-warning messages through local radio and SMS [S1, Early warning and community engagement]
- `DR-W2` Deploy trained volunteers to engage communities and support early-warning dissemination [S1, Early warning and community engagement]
- `DR-W3` Carry out a rapid assessment of water facility functionality [S1, WASH]
- `DR-W4` Activate community water committees [S1, WASH]
- `DR-W5` Advocate for expanding and protecting fallback grazing areas [S1, Livelihoods]
- `DR-W6` Promote household water conservation, water storage and rainwater harvesting [S3, Drought, pp. 44-45]

**Warning** - 3-month rain 25-50% of normal (comparable to NDMA 'Alarm')

- `DR-A1` Repair and rehabilitate water facilities (e.g. boreholes) [S1, WASH]
- `DR-A2` Desilt water pans and repair earth dams [S1, WASH]
- `DR-A3` Install mass water storage tanks [S1, WASH]
- `DR-A4` Provide cash for WASH to targeted households [S1, WASH / Cash]
- `DR-A5` Provide fodder for priority livestock through commodity vouchers [S1, Livelihoods]
- `DR-A6` Procure and distribute drought-tolerant seeds before the next planting season [S1, Livelihoods]
- `DR-A7` Encourage early de-stocking and saving fodder for key animals [S3, Drought, p. 47]

**Emergency** - 3-month rain below 25% of normal (comparable to NDMA 'Emergency')

- `DR-E1` Unconditional cash transfers to the most affected pastoral households [S6, Anticipatory action description]
- `DR-E2` Livestock health services (treatment and vaccination) to protect core herds [S6, Anticipatory action description]
- `DR-E3` Identify and assist vulnerable community members [S3, Drought, p. 43]
- `DR-E4` Plan a basic food ration (about 2,100 kcal per person per day) for vulnerable households [S3, Drought, p. 43]
- `DR-E5` Continue repair of critical water facilities and cash for WASH [S1, WASH]
- `DR-E6` Hold community review meetings and post-trigger surveys to adjust the response [S1, Coordination and learning]

## Flood

**Watch** - 3-day rain (observed or forecast) at or above the county's 95th percentile

- `FL-W1` Disseminate targeted early warnings to at-risk communities through trained volunteers [S2, Early actions / DRR]
- `FL-W2` Place or check flood depth markers at crossing points (cash for work) [S2, Early actions]
- `FL-W3` Identify evacuation sites [S2, Physical evacuation]
- `FL-W4` Pre-position shelter and WASH relief items [S2, Early actions]
- `FL-W5` Advise households to store valuables above flood level and keep extra drinking water [S3, Floods, pp. 100, 102]

**Warning** - 3-day rain at or above the county's 99th percentile

- `FL-A1` Give cash grants or vouchers so vulnerable people can evacuate early [S2, Physical evacuation]
- `FL-A2` Support physical evacuation to identified sites [S5, Disaster risk reduction]
- `FL-A3` Vaccinate or treat livestock through community animal health workers [S2, Early actions]
- `FL-A4` Deploy emergency water treatment and sensitise communities on household water treatment [S2, WASH]
- `FL-A5` Tell people to evacuate immediately when advised, or move to higher ground [S3, Floods, pp. 102-103]
- `FL-A6` Warn against walking or driving through flowing water and underpasses ('turn around, don't drown') [S3, Floods, p. 103]

**Emergency** - 3-day rain at or above the county's 99.9th percentile, or flooding reported

- `FL-E1` Distribute shelter and non-food item kits to displaced households [S2, Shelter]
- `FL-E2` Provide multipurpose cash grants to affected households [S2, Cash / basic needs]
- `FL-E3` Deploy health surge teams for disease outbreak management [S2, Health]
- `FL-E4` Run emergency water treatment plants for affected communities [S2, WASH]
- `FL-E5` After waters recede: avoid floodwater, clean and disinfect homes and water sources [S3, Floods, pp. 103-104]
- `FL-E6` Hold community review meetings to review ongoing implementation [S5, Cross cutting]

## Cyclone

**Watch** - Cyclone season (Nov-Apr) in a county with recorded storm exposure, or a storm reported in the South-West Indian Ocean by RSMC La Reunion/KMD

- `CY-W1` Track official cyclone information and follow early-warning advisories (KMD, RSMC La Reunion) [S3, School safety, storm rules, p. 144]
- `CY-W2` Assess risk and make a household and community cyclone plan [S3, Tropical cyclones, p. 94]
- `CY-W3` Do mitigation work: brace roofs, fit shutters, trim trees [S3, Tropical cyclones, p. 95]
- `CY-W4` Practise evacuation routes and stock emergency supplies [S3, Tropical cyclones, p. 96]
- `CY-W5` Disseminate early-warning messages to coastal communities [S4, Anticipatory actions]

**Warning** - Only when KMD or RSMC La Reunion has issued an official warning for the Kenyan coast (this tool never forecasts storm paths)

- `CY-A1` Secure loose objects and bring animals indoors [S3, Tropical cyclones, p. 96]
- `CY-A2` Make sure people know when and where to evacuate, prioritising those in temporary structures [S3, Tropical cyclones, p. 97]
- `CY-A3` Provide anticipatory cash transfers so households can prepare [S4, Anticipatory actions]
- `CY-A4` Support household food storage and evacuation planning [S4, Anticipatory actions]
- `CY-A5` Relocate people and assets and safeguard critical infrastructure [S4, Anticipatory actions]

**Emergency** - Storm impacts occurring or imminent per official warnings

- `CY-E1` Shelter in place away from windows [S3, Tropical cyclones, p. 97]
- `CY-E2` Stay sheltered during the calm eye of the storm [S3, Tropical cyclones, p. 97]
- `CY-E3` After the storm: give first aid and check buildings for damage [S3, Tropical cyclones, p. 97]
- `CY-E4` Avoid floodwater, electrical hazards and contaminated food or water [S3, Tropical cyclones, p. 97]
- `CY-E5` Provide in-kind food where markets are not functioning [S4, Anticipatory actions]
